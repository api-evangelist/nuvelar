# Nuvelar

> Nuvelar is an Argentine technology solutions firm founded in 2014 that designs and builds digital experiences across consulting, hardware, software, and design. Its flagship product is Nuvelar Display, a cloud platform for creating, distributing, and managing digital-signage content networks, alongside custom interactive installations (videowalls, interactive tables, and branded experiential builds). Nuvelar does not currently publish a public API or developer portal.

## About
- [Nuvelar website](https://nuvelar.com): Company homepage — products, services, and portfolio.
- [Customer login](https://www.nuvelar.com/public/login): Sign-in for the Nuvelar Display content-management platform.

## Products
- Nuvelar Display: Cloud-based digital-signage platform — content scheduling, player monitoring, dynamic templates, multi-format support, and social/weather/RSS integrations.
- Custom development & consulting: Interactive displays, videowall installations, and bespoke software for experiential projects.
